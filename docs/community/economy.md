# Economy

**Economy** gives your server its own currency members can earn and spend on items you define — badges, perks, roles, whatever fits your community.

## Setting it up

1. Enable **Economy** from [Modules](/dashboard/modules-page).
2. Open **Economy → Shop** and add an item: name, price, and optionally a stock limit or a role it grants on purchase.

::: tip Shop items save immediately
Unlike most settings, adding or editing a shop item doesn't go through the draft/deploy flow — it's saved and live as soon as you confirm it.
:::

## What members see

Members earn currency through server activity and can view and spend it through Veyra's economy commands in Discord.
