# Levels

**Levels** tracks member activity (text and voice) and awards experience and levels over time, optionally with roles granted automatically as members level up.

## Setting it up

1. Enable **Levels** from [Modules](/dashboard/modules-page).
2. Open **Levels → Settings** to adjust XP rate, cooldown between XP awards, and where level-up announcements post.
3. Open the **Rewards** tab to grant a role automatically at a chosen level — pick a level, pick a role, click **Add reward**.
4. Click **Save draft** on the Rewards tab (it has its own save button, separate from Settings), then [deploy](/dashboard/draft-deploy-publish).

## Leaderboard

The **Overview** tab shows a live leaderboard of your most active members by total XP.
