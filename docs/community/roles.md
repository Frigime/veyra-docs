# Roles

**Roles** lets members give themselves roles from a panel you design — hobby tags, pronoun roles, notification opt-ins, whatever you want available on demand.

## What you'll need first

A text channel for the panel, and at least one role members are allowed to self-assign.

![The Roles configure tab, editing a self-role panel](/screenshots/community/roles-configure.png)

## Setting it up

1. Enable **Roles** from [Modules](/dashboard/modules-page).
2. Open **Roles → Configure**, click **New role panel**.
3. Pick a publication channel, add role options (each with a label and emoji), and choose **Buttons** or **Select menu** as the interaction style.
4. Choose whether members can pick multiple roles (**Stackable**) or only one at a time (**Exclusive**).
5. Save the draft, then [deploy](/dashboard/draft-deploy-publish).
6. Click **Publish** to post the panel to Discord.

::: warning Deleting a panel doesn't delete its Discord message
If you remove a panel from the dashboard, the message it already posted in Discord stays there — Veyra doesn't have permission to reach back and delete arbitrary messages. Delete it directly in Discord if you no longer want it visible.
:::
