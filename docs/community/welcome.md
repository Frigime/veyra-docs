# Welcome

**Welcome** greets new members when they join your server, with a customizable message posted to a channel of your choice.

## Setting it up

1. Enable **Welcome** from [Modules](/dashboard/modules-page).
2. Open **Welcome**, choose the channel to post in.
3. Write your message. You can use variables like `{user}` and `{server}` that Veyra fills in automatically for each new member.
4. Save the draft, then [deploy](/dashboard/draft-deploy-publish).

## See also

[Goodbye](/community/goodbye) for the departure equivalent, and [Verification](/community/verification) if you want new members to prove they're human before they can chat.
