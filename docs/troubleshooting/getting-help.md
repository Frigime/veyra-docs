# Getting Help

If this documentation didn't answer your question, reach out through wherever you got Veyra — that's the fastest way to reach someone who can actually help with your specific installation.

## Before you ask

Have this ready, so whoever's helping doesn't have to ask for it first:

- **How you installed Veyra** (Windows, Linux/macOS/VPS, Docker, or Pterodactyl).
- **The exact error message or behavior**, word for word if possible.
- What [System Health](/dashboard/system-health) shows.
