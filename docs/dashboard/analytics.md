# Analytics

**Analytics** is a read-only reporting page, not a module you enable — it's part of the dashboard itself and always available.

It gives you a picture of your server's activity over time: growth, command usage, and how your enabled modules are being used, over the last 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.

There's nothing to configure here — just open **Overview → Analytics** in the sidebar whenever you want a snapshot of how your server is doing.
