# Bot Settings

**Bot Settings** controls how your bot presents itself on Discord — its status message and activity, independent of any single server.

![The Bot Settings presence card, controlling the bot's Discord status](/screenshots/dashboard/bot-settings.png)

## What you can set

- **Presence** — the "Playing / Watching / Listening to…" text shown under your bot's name in Discord's member list (for example, *Watching over the server*).
- **Streaming URL** — if you set your presence type to Streaming, the Twitch/YouTube URL Discord links to.

## Changing your bot's credentials later

Bot Settings does not include changing your Application ID, Client Secret, or Bot Token — those are entered once during [Connecting Your Discord Application](/first-run/connecting-discord). If you ever need to point Veyra at a completely different Discord application, see [Resetting First-Run Setup](/maintenance/resetting-setup).

Open **Configuration → Bot Settings** in the sidebar.
